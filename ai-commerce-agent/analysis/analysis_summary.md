# Analysis Results Summary
## AI E-commerce Assistant Experience Evaluation

## 📊 **Key Findings**

### **Overall Results**

**Sample Size:**
- 6 participants completed both pre-test and post-test
- Pre-test: 34 questions
- Post-test: 16 questions

**Overall Score Improvement:**
- **Pre-test mean score:** 1.76 (out of 5)
- **Post-test mean score:** 2.81 (out of 5)
- **Improvement:** +1.05 points (+59.4% increase)

**Statistical Significance:**
- Paired t-test: t = 21.647, p = 0.0500
- **Not statistically significant** at α = 0.05 (exactly at the threshold)
- Note: With only 6 participants, statistical power is limited

## 💡 **Key Takeaways**

### **Strengths:**
1. **Multilingual Support** - Excellent performance, meeting very high expectations
2. **Recommendation Accuracy** - Excellent, meeting high expectations
3. **Overall Experience** - Strong positive impact on shopping experience

### **Areas for Improvement:**
1. **Visual Identification** - Could be more prominent/easier to identify
2. **Explanation Clarity** - Some participants want clearer explanations
3. **Privacy Assurance** - Need to better address privacy concerns

### **Limitations:**
- Small sample size (n=6) limits statistical power
- Results may not be generalizable to larger populations
- P-value exactly at 0.05 threshold suggests need for larger study
